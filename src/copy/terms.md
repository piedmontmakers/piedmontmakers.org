## SMS Terms

Piedmont Makers offers an optional text messaging program, Piedmont Makers Robotics Tournament Updates, for the robotics tournaments we host. Messages are sent by **Piedmont Makers**, the registered brand for this program.

**What we send.** Tournament updates for teams, families, volunteers, and attendees, such as event schedules, match and queueing reminders, venue and logistics updates, results, and announcements about upcoming Piedmont Makers robotics events, plus occasional messages about supporting Piedmont Makers.

**How you sign up.** You opt in by giving us your mobile number and agreeing to receive texts, for example on a registration or sign-up form, or by texting a keyword to our number. Signing up is voluntary. Your consent to receive text messages is not a condition of registering for, attending, or donating to any program or event.

**How often.** Message frequency varies. You'll get more messages on tournament days and few or none in between.

**Cost.** Message and data rates may apply. Check your mobile plan for details. Piedmont Makers doesn't charge for these messages.

**Opting out.** Reply **STOP** to any message at any time to stop receiving texts. We'll also honor CANCEL, END, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE, and similar replies. You'll get one final message confirming you've been unsubscribed, and no further messages after that. To sign up again, reply **START** or opt in again the way you did originally.

**Getting help.** Reply **HELP** to any message for help, or email **hello@piedmontmakers.org**.

**Carriers.** Supported on most major U.S. carriers. Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

**Your phone number.** You agree that the number you provide is yours or that you have permission from the account holder to sign it up. If you're under 18, please get a parent's or guardian's permission first. Let us know if your number changes or you give it up, so texts don't go to someone else.

**Privacy.** We use your mobile number only to send the messages you signed up for. We don't sell it, and we don't share mobile numbers or text messaging opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy#text-messages) for more.
