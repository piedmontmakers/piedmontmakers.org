More than 1,000 kids across the East Bay are on a Piedmont Makers team this fall. Here's where they landed:

- **514 in LEGO League Explore**, grades K-3
- **395 in LEGO League Challenge**, grades 4-8
- **90 in FTC**, grades 7-12
- **85 in FRC**, grades 9-12

Every one of those teams needs two adults to run it. Nearly 200 parents and mentors came through August coach training for Explore and Challenge, most of them signing up to lead a program they had never seen before. Teams are already meeting at coaches' houses.

## This season is FIRST CANOPY

FIRST built the year around biodiversity, with a different game at each level.

Both LEGO League levels run **BIOGLOW**, set in the rainforest, on biodiversity and habitat recovery. Challenge teams add an Innovation Project on top of the robot game: pick a problem, research it, pitch a solution to judges.

FTC teams get **BIOBUZZ** at kickoff on September 12. FRC gets **BIOCORE** on January 9, 2027, which is when Highlander Robotics 8033 starts build season.

## Dates for the fridge

Explore and Challenge teams meet through the fall and finish in December at the Explore Festival and the Community Tournament. FTC league meets start in November. Every date is on the [calendar](/calendar).
