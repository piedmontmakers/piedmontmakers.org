Piedmont Unified School District has started its search for a new superintendent, and the district asked us to pass this invitation along to our community. The district's search firm, Leadership Associates, is running a set of community listening sessions this week. They are the main way parents and community members can give input on the search.

Leadership Associates will compile and analyze what it hears and share it with the Board of Education, and the Board will use that feedback when it interviews candidates. Many Piedmont Makers families have kids in PUSD schools, and many of our programs run in district spaces like the Engineering Lab at Piedmont High School, so we hope you'll add your voice.

## The sessions

No RSVP needed. Each session is aimed at one school community, but you're welcome at any of them.

- **Havens, Wildwood, and Beach families:** Wednesday, October 7, 6:30 to 7:15 pm, Havens library
- **Piedmont Middle School families:** Wednesday, October 7, 7:30 to 8:15 pm, PMS library
- **All community, virtual:** Thursday, October 8, 7:30 to 8:15 pm, [join on Zoom](https://us06web.zoom.us/j/76996405560?pwd=86n8KL9xOvH7d4zgqou0GzrRZ5kibI.1)
- **Piedmont High School and Millennium High School families:** Friday, October 9, 9:30 to 10:15 am, Alan Harvey Theater lobby

## What they'll ask

Leadership Associates consultants will open with an overview of the search process, then ask two questions:

1. What qualities, characteristics, background, and professional and personal experiences do you want in the next superintendent?
2. What do you see as the district's strengths, and the major challenges facing it in the years ahead?

## Can't make it?

The same two questions are in an [anonymous online survey](https://www.surveymonkey.com/r/WTL3ZYP), so you can respond on your own time.
