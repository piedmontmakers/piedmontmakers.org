On Saturday, October 24, 60 LEGO League Challenge teams spent the day at Piedmont Middle School's Morrison Gym for our robot game practice. Each team ran its robot twice on official game tables, with referees scoring and an announcer calling the rounds.

Scores were for practice only, so kids could get comfortable with tournament conditions before December: setting up at a competition table, launching their robot, and running missions in front of a crowd of parents and other teams.

Teams also watched each other's runs, which gave them a look at how others are approaching this season's BIOGLOW missions and some strategy ideas to bring back to their own tables.

Thanks to all the volunteers who refereed and helped run the day.
