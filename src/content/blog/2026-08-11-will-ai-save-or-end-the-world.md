Piedmont Boosters, the Piedmont Parents Network, and Piedmont Makers are teaming up for a community evening about the technology reshaping how we live and work.

**Saturday, September 26, 2026**
**Alan Harvey Theater, Piedmont High School**
**Doors open 6:00 PM · Presentation begins 6:30 PM**

We'll screen *The AI Doc*, a documentary we've licensed for the evening, followed by a discussion with a panel of experts and audience Q&A.

Our panel of Piedmont residents:

- **Jeff Bleich**, Chief Legal Officer at Anthropic
- **Marco Casalaina**, AI Futurist at Microsoft
- **Clara Shih**, SVP of Business AI at Meta
- **Emily Chang** (moderator), Executive Producer at Bloomberg Media

The panel discussion will be recorded and posted to the City of Piedmont and PUSD websites for later viewing.

**[Buy tickets](https://piedmontstore.org/products/ai-will-it-save-or-end-the-world)** for $50, a tax-deductible donation to Piedmont Boosters. We hope to see you there.
