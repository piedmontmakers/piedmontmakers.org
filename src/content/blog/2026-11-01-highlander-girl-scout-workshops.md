Every year, Highlander Robotics students run workshops for Girl Scouts, from Daisies on up. Team members write the presentations and build the materials themselves, giving scouts a way to earn STEAM and robotics-related badges.

Two workshops are coming up this season at the team's 10th Street practice field in Oakland. Girl Scouts will earn badges, and you don't need to be a Girl Scout to come: both workshops are open to any girl in the grade range.

## Nightlight Design, for Brownies (grades 2-3)

- **When:** Saturday, November 14, 10 AM to 12 PM
- **Where:** 3100 E 10th St, Oakland
- **Cost:** $25 registration fee

[Sign up for Nightlight Design](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdfA9wV7rjYvBZjY20xLorvwRhOM68uCqbYRzeGvz5IXFvo5w/viewform)

## Arduino Project, for Cadettes (grades 6-8)

- **When:** Saturday, December 5, 10 AM to 1 PM
- **Where:** 3100 E 10th St, Oakland
- **Cost:** $25 registration fee

[Sign up for the Arduino Project](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScqjtiG2CUhGp5OaSJ6et8cUzCxv_c1xmzisWsyNDVk-YeM_g/viewform)

Payment instructions are on each sign-up form.

## About Highlander Robotics

Highlander Robotics is the high school FIRST Robotics Competition program behind Teams 8033 and 8433. These workshops are one way its students pass along what they've learned to younger kids. You can find more about the team at [frc8033.com](https://frc8033.com).
